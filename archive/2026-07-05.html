At the mirror

Ben, notice something for a second — that flash of irritation, that tightening in your chest when something sets you off. For most of your life it's felt like a command, hasn't it? Like the feeling shows up and you're already moving before you've even decided anything. But here's what's actually true: that trigger is a messenger knocking on the door, not a general barking an order — it's telling you something matters to you, and then it's stepping back to let *you* choose what happens next. That gap between the feeling and the response, that's where your whole life is built, one small decision at a time. So plant your feet, look at yourself, and say it slowly: "My thoughts shape my reality, and my choices shape who I'm becoming. My triggers are information, not orders." Say it again if you need to, and mean it.
